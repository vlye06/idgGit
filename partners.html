Our Manufacturing Partners

We represent world-class machinery brands at every stage of textile production — each chosen for engineering excellence, reliability and after-sales support.

IDG-Weave acts as the exclusive or authorized regional representative for eight manufacturers whose equipment spans the full textile production chain — from spinning raw fiber to climate-controlled finishing. Click any partner below to visit their official website.

01 Spinning

Fiber to Yarn

Converting raw natural or synthetic fibers into yarns ready for weaving.

Spinning Machinery

Marzoli

A Camozzi Group company and global leader in complete spinning lines. Marzoli manufactures blow-room systems, cards, draw frames, lap formers, combers, roving frames and ring spinning machines — delivering full-plant solutions from fiber opening to finished yarn.

en.marzoli.camozzi.com
02 Sizing & Warping

Beam Preparation

Warping and sizing the yarn onto beams before it enters the loom.

Warping & Sizing Machines

Icomatex

Belgian manufacturer of warping and sizing equipment. Icomatex produces direct warpers, sectional warpers, ball warpers, creels, sizing lines for all fiber types, re-beamers and open-width systems — covering every beam preparation requirement for weaving mills.

icomatex.com
03 Weaving

Yarn to Fabric

High-speed airjet and rapier looms that interlace warp and weft into finished cloth.

Airjet & Rapier Looms

Picanol

Belgian weaving technology leader with over 85 years of innovation. Picanol’s airjet and rapier machines are used in mills worldwide for apparel, home textiles and technical fabrics — renowned for high speed, reliability, and low energy consumption per meter woven.

picanol.be
04 – 05 Wet Processing

Dyeing & Washing

Chemical and thermal treatment of fabric for color, cleanliness and preparation for finishing.

Dyeing & Washing Systems

PROSMH

Specialist in industrial wet-processing machinery, PROSMH offers KTM Krantz dyeing systems, rope dye ranges for denim, open-width continuous washing lines and ultrasonic treatment technology — covering both dyeing and washing / bleaching / mercerizing needs in a single partnership.

prosmh.com
06 Stenters

Finishing & Heat-Setting

Width, dimensional stability and surface finish are locked in at the stenter frame.

Stenter Frames & Finishing

ERBATECH

Turkish manufacturer of stenter frames and textile finishing equipment. ERBATECH stenters feature multi-zone heating, precise width and overfeed control, energy-efficient air circulation and are suited to wovens, knits and technical textiles across all fiber compositions.

erbatech.com
07 Quality Control

Inspection & Packing

End-of-line inspection, rolling, folding, palletizing and packaging of finished fabric.

Inspection, Rolling & Packing Machines

Demsan

Turkish manufacturer of fabric-handling and packing machinery. Demsan’s range covers fabric rolling and batching machines, plaiting machines, single- and double-fold palletizers, fabric spreading tables, packaging lines and beam winding units — ensuring every meter leaving the mill is perfectly presented.

demsan.com.tr
08 Laboratory

Testing & Measurement

Instruments to verify yarn and fabric quality against international standards at every process step.

Textile & Spinning Lab Instruments

Tomsic

Italian distributor and integrator of laboratory testing instruments for textiles, spinning and specialty industries. Tomsic’s portfolio covers tensile strength testers, color fastness equipment, yarn evenness and hairiness testers, pilling and abrasion testers — providing complete process-control metrology for quality-focused mills.

tomsic.it
09 Climate & Waste

Environmental Control

Keeping the factory floor at the right temperature, humidity and air quality while safely handling lint and dust.

Climate & Waste Control Systems

Tüfekçi

Turkish specialist in factory climate control and textile waste management. Tüfekçi systems include precision humidification units, air-conditioning solutions tailored for weaving and spinning environments, and lint & dust collection filtration systems — protecting both machinery life and worker health.

tufekci.com.tr
